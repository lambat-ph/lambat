How we checked

We compared the ranking with flood events from the news.

Why we cannot confirm the ranking yet

The ranking shows no detectable agreement with 13 news-reported flood events (Spearman -0.10, 95% CI -0.48 to 0.30). News reports mostly follow where people live (0.64 with population density), so they are a weak ruler. So: cannot confirm yet.

Read the validation notes

Other limits: access is modelled from maps, not checked on the ground. Each dose point needs a prescriber. We report residents within reach of care, never lives saved.
