    Can we trust the ranking?

    Not yet confirmed. Checked against 13 flood events named in the news, the ranking shows no detectable agreement (Spearman -0.10, 95% CI -0.48 to 0.30). News reports mostly follow where people live, so they are a weak ruler. Read the validation notes.

    Other limits: access is modelled from maps, not checked on the ground. Each dose point needs a prescriber. We report residents within reach of care, never lives saved.
